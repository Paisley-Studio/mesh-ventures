    Good to know

    Frequently asked

    What does the KES 50,000 starting price include?

    The Essential package covers planning consultation, a themed décor setup, event-day coordination and set-up/teardown for up to 50 guests. Catering, entertainment, photography and other extras can be added in the budget planner.

    Is the price the same for every event type?

    Every event type starts from KES 50,000. Your final quote depends on the package, number of guests, venue and the extras you choose.

    How do I secure my date?

    Place a booking request, then we confirm availability and send a quotation. A 30% deposit holds your date, and the balance is settled before the event. Payment details are shared with your confirmation.

    Can you plan a ruracio with our family customs?

    Yes. We coordinate seating, décor, MC and programme around your family and community traditions, and work closely with the elders and organisers.

    Do you work outside Nairobi?

    Yes, we plan events in surrounding counties and across Kenya. Transport and accommodation for our team may be added for distant venues.

    Can I change my date or package later?

    Changes are possible subject to availability. Tell us as early as you can and we'll do our best to accommodate you.
